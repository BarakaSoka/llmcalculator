# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Critique code, not
people.

## Standards

Things that make this project work:

- Being welcoming to people whose experience level differs from yours
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change, and that a contributor may
  disagree with a review
- Focusing on what is best for people using the tool
- Saying "I was wrong" without ceremony when you were

Things that do not belong here:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/BarakaSoka/llmcalculator/security/advisories/new),
which is a private channel, or by contacting the maintainer directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments,
close issues or PRs, or block accounts, and will explain the reason when doing
so is appropriate.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith may themselves face consequences from
other maintainers.
